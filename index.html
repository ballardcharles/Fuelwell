Validus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short: everything you log stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, so we never receive your workouts, food, weight or health data. The only things that leave your phone are food searches (to look up nutrition) and what the advertising provider collects, both described below.

What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, food log, saved foods and meals, water, weigh-ins, body measurements, goals and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use Export backup or the CSV export in Settings, a file is created that you choose where to save or send; we never see it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the app can:

  • Read your weight, active calories, workouts, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and sleep, to fill in your log and show your recovery and readiness.
  • Write your weigh-ins, workouts (with their calories) and water, so they appear in Health and count toward your Activity rings.

Health data is used only inside the app to provide these features. It is never sent to us, never shared with or sold to anyone, and never used for advertising. You choose exactly which types the app may read or write, and can change that at any time in the iPhone’s Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Food search and barcodes

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent directly from your phone to the free public food databases USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to look up nutrition. Like any website, they receive your IP address with the request. Nothing else about you is sent. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Camera

The camera is used only to read a barcode when you tap the scan button. Barcode detection happens on your device; no images are saved or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google’s ad software may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn’t shared and ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. No health, workout or food data is ever passed to the ad provider.

Notifications and alarms

Daily reminders, the rest-timer alarm and the lock-screen timer are scheduled on your phone by iOS. No notification content passes through any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Disconnect or limit Apple Health access in the iPhone’s Health settings.
  • Turn off tracking for ads in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Delete all your data at any time by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
